An entry for the make.fun bounty "The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary".

If one word had to be erased from the dictionary forever, this entry picks the
word that turns every want into a guilt trip. Delete it and the language loses
its favourite way of telling people what they ought to do — every lecture and
every chore list suddenly runs out of words.

